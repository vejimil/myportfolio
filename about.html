01 Origin story

I've loved games since I was very young — from Nintendo to PlayStation to PC — and they've stayed close ever since. They shaped how I think, not only about play, but about how emotion, structure, rhythm, and interaction are designed.

That naturally led me toward interactive design. But I'm just as energized by fashion, foreign languages, and music. Moving between different interests changes how I see beauty, people, and experience.

02 Side quests

A lot of my growth has come from stepping into spaces I wasn't fully ready for and deciding to learn by doing.

Stepping into fashion

I joined my school's graduation fashion show before I knew how to make clothes. Instead of waiting until I felt ready, I contacted the professor directly and asked for a chance. Through steady effort I completed three looks and later became planning team leader. It taught me that interest only becomes meaningful when it's carried by action, discipline, and responsibility.

Designing fun on a tight budget

I helped plan a university festival, working on games and events with a limited budget. I got interested in creating raw, immediate fun from simple ideas — like human curling with wheeled tubs and carefully prepared safety measures. Memorable experiences don't need scale or expensive technology; they come from understanding people, energy, rules, and surprise.

Small acts that matter

I've also spent small pockets of time volunteering at meal services and donating meal vouchers. Quiet work, but meaningful — the way we speak, listen, and treat people shapes collaboration as much as technical skill does.

03 How I play (with a team)

  • Carry it through. When something is mine, I want to understand it properly and finish it to a standard I'm proud of.
  • Go deep. I study enough to do the work well, not just enough to finish it.
  • Listen first. I try to learn my teammates' fields and approach collaboration with respect. Good teamwork starts from listening well, not speaking first.

04 What keeps me curious

Fashion, languages, music, and games aren't separate interests to me — together they're the energy that keeps me moving. I care about happiness in everyday life and try to be kind to the people around me. If one thoughtful word can make someone's day lighter, I think that matters, and I want both the things I make and the way I work to carry that warmth.
